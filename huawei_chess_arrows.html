Privacy Policy for Chess Arrows

Last updated: 20/10/2026

Chess Arrows (“the app”, “we”) is an Android app that lets you draw arrows on top of other apps, for example to plan or explain chess moves. It is published by Geo Rao (“the developer”). This policy applies to the version distributed through Huawei AppGallery and explains, in plain words, what the app does with information.

Contact: geo.rao.00007@gmail.com

1. The short version

  • The app only draws arrows. It cannot see, read or record your screen, and it does not read what is in the apps underneath it.
  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location or contact list.
  • Your settings stay on your device.
  • This version of the app does not show ads and does not use any advertising service or advertising identifier.
  • To adjust some app settings remotely, the app uses Firebase Remote Config from Google, which receives limited technical information described in section 4.

2. Permissions the app uses

PermissionWhy
Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) To draw the floating pencil, the arrows and the optional board grid on top of other apps. This is the core feature of the app and the permission is used for nothing else. You grant it yourself in system settings and can revoke it at any time.
Foreground service To keep the overlay running while you use other apps. A notification is shown while it runs, with Clear and Stop buttons.
Notifications To show that notification (Android 13 and later).
Internet To load the remote settings described below.

The app does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, accessibility services or screen-recording (screen capture) features.

3. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your device. We never receive it.

  • your arrow style (color, thickness, opacity) and the position of the floating buttons;
  • the mode you chose (Default or Advanced) and, for Advanced mode, the position and size of the 8×8 board grid you placed, saved separately for portrait and landscape;
  • your language choice and whether you have finished the introduction;
  • a counter of how many times the app was opened.

The arrows you draw exist only on the screen while the overlay is on. They are not saved or sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes all of this.

4. Third-party services

Firebase Remote Config and Firebase Installations (remote settings)

Used to switch app features on or off without releasing a new version. According to Google, Remote Config receives your country code, language code, time zone, OS version, the app ID and package name, and the SDK version, together with your IP address as part of the network request; Firebase Installations creates a random per-installation identifier. This does not identify you personally and is not used for advertising. The data is sent to Google, which may process it outside your country. More: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not receive any data that identifies you.

5. How information is used, and our legal basis

We use this information only to provide the app and keep it working. Where the law of your country requires a legal basis (for example the GDPR in the EU and UK), this relies on our legitimate interest in providing a working app.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Device data: you can clear the app’s saved settings in Android: Settings → Apps → Chess Arrows → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling.
  • Permission: you can remove the Display over other apps permission at any time in Android settings; the overlay will then stop working.
  • Rights: depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal data, to object to its use, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Since we hold no account or personal profile about you, there is generally nothing we can look up; the configuration provider above holds the limited data it receives. You can also write to us at the contact email above.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where that is the local age of digital consent), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help.

8. Retention and security

We keep no personal data of our own. Data held by the service provider is kept under its policy and is encrypted in transit. Settings on your device are protected by Android’s app sandbox.

9. International transfers

Google may process the data described in section 4 in countries other than your own, under its own safeguards.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app uses information, for example by adding advertising to this version, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect, and update the AppGallery listing.
