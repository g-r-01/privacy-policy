Privacy Policy for Chess Arrows

Last updated: 10/12/2026

Chess Arrows (“the app”, “we”) is an Android app that lets you draw arrows on top of other apps, for example to plan or explain chess moves. It is published by Geo Rao (“the developer”). This policy explains what the app does with information, in plain words.

Contact: geo.rao.00007@gmail.com

1. The short version

  • The app only draws arrows. It cannot see, read or record your screen, and it does not read what is in the apps underneath it.
  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number or contact list.
  • Your settings stay on your device.
  • The app is free and shows ads. To show ads and to adjust some app settings remotely, it uses services from Google (AdMob and Firebase) and, on Huawei devices, Huawei. These services collect some device information, described in section 4.
  • If you are in the EU, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time (section 6).

2. Permissions the app uses

Permission Why
Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) To draw the floating pencil, the arrows and the optional board grid on top of other apps. Used for nothing else.
Foreground service To keep the overlay running while you use other apps. A notification is shown while it runs, with Clear and Stop buttons.
Notifications To show that notification (Android 13 and later).
Internet To load ads and the remote settings described below.

The app does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, accessibility services or screen-recording (screen capture) features.

3. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your device. We never receive it.

  • your arrow style (color, thickness, opacity) and the position of the floating buttons;
  • the mode you chose (Default or Advanced) and, for Advanced mode, the position and size of the 8×8 board grid you placed, saved separately for portrait and landscape;
  • your language choice and whether you have finished the introduction;
  • a counter of how many times the app was opened (used to decide when an ad may be shown), and the time of the last review prompt.

The arrows you draw exist only on the screen while the overlay is on. They are not saved or sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes all of this.

4. Third-party services

The app includes the following services. They run on your device and may collect data under their own privacy policies.

Google AdMob (advertising)

Used on devices with Google services to show ads (a full-screen ad when the app opens, and an ad on the main screen). According to Google, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects, and uses for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention: IP address (which can be used to estimate your general location), app interactions such as app launches and taps, diagnostic information such as app launch time and hang rate, and device and account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Data is encrypted in transit. More: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google User Messaging Platform (consent)

In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent tool asks for your choices about ads and stores them on your device.

Firebase Remote Config and Firebase Installations (remote settings)

Used to switch app features, such as whether ads are shown, without releasing a new version. According to Google, Remote Config receives your country code, language code, time zone, OS version, the app ID and package name, and the SDK version; Firebase Installations creates a random per-installation identifier. This does not identify you personally. More: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Google Play In-App Review

The app may occasionally ask you to rate it. Google shows and handles that dialog under Google’s privacy policy; we do not receive your rating or identity through it.

Huawei Ads and Huawei Mobile Services (Huawei devices only)

On Huawei devices without Google services, ads are provided by Huawei Ads, which may collect device identifiers (such as an advertising ID) and ad interaction data under Huawei’s privacy policy: https://consumer.huawei.com/en/legal/privacy-policy/

We do not sell your personal data. We do not receive the data above in a form that identifies you; it goes to the service providers listed here.

5. How information is used, and our legal basis

We use this information only to (a) provide the app and keep it working, (b) show ads that fund the app, and (c) prevent fraud and abuse. Where the law of your country requires a legal basis (for example the GDPR in the EU and UK): ads that use device identifiers rely on your consent; running the app and remote settings rely on our legitimate interest in providing a working app.

6. Your choices

  • Ad choices in the EU, UK and Switzerland: when the app first needs to show ads you will see a consent form. You can change your answer at any time using Ad privacy settings on the app’s main screen (shown where it applies to you).
  • Advertising ID: you can reset it or opt out of ad personalization in Android: Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads.
  • Device data: you can clear the app’s saved settings in Android: Settings → Apps → Chess Arrows → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling.
  • Rights: depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal data, to object to its use, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Since we hold no account or personal profile about you, there is generally nothing we can look up; the ad and configuration providers above hold the data they collect, and you can use their own privacy tools. You can also write to us at the contact email above.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where that is the local age of digital consent), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help.

8. Retention and security

We keep no personal data of our own. Data held by the service providers is kept under their policies. Data sent by the SDKs is encrypted in transit. Settings on your device are protected by Android’s app sandbox.

9. International transfers

Google and Huawei may process data in countries other than your own, under their own safeguards.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app uses information, we will update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes we will also update the Google Play listing.
